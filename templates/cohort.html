Over the study (median and middle 50%, per study week)

Each person's week is averaged first (needs 3 values that week). Weeks with fewer than 5 people in a group are hidden. Later weeks include fewer people (dropout), so the tail is not a trend. Lines that stay apart mean a baseline difference, not a change over time. A night counts in the week of the morning it ended.

About these measures

Sleep measures come from Oura's nightly sleep record and use only the main night (the long sleep record with 3–16 h in bed and 1–14 h asleep); heart rate, HRV and SpO₂ are Oura's overnight values from the same main nights. Bedtime variability is each night's 2-week bedtime SD, so a patient's value is their typical 2-week bedtime SD. Steps, rest regularity, night rest and rest midpoint use days with ≥80% ring wear; the last three are estimates from Oura's 5-minute activity classes. A patient counts with at least 14 nights (or valid days) of a measure. Missing nights are never counted as zero. Clinical labels were recorded once at enrollment and there are no clinical event dates. See the trajectories notebook for the full method.
