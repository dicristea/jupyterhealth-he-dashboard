Sleep and activity

How these values are chosen Main night: the night's long sleep record with 3–16 h in bed and 1–14 h asleep. Naps and short rest records are left out. Sleep cards, HRV, heart rate and SpO₂ use main nights (Oura measures them overnight). Steps and rest regularity use days with ≥80% ring wear. Recent = median of the last {{ p.window }} study days (needs {{ p.min_recent }} nights; bedtime variability needs {{ p.bed_sd_min }}). Usual range = middle 50% of the nights before that, needing ≥{{ p.min_base }}. A missing night is never counted as zero.

One bar per night, from bedtime (top) to waking (bottom); the dot is the midpoint. Green band = this patient's usual midpoint (middle 50% of earlier nights). Lighter bars are shorter or daytime sleep records rather than a main night (shown at the bottom edge if they end after 16:00). Click a night to see its sleep stages.

Stages use Oura's 5-minute coding (1 deep, 2 light, 3 REM, 4 awake), which matches Oura's nightly totals in this file; confirm with the study team before clinical use.

Sleep measures come from a consumer ring (Oura) and are not validated for diagnosing HE or sleep disorders. Use them to prompt a conversation, not to make decisions.

Comparisons are with this patient's own usual nights, not a normal range. A missing night means no sleep record, not poor sleep.

Clinical values are from enrollment only. This dataset has no dates of HE episodes, admissions, lab or medication changes, so a sleep change can't be linked to an event here. Times are clock times only; there are no calendar dates.
